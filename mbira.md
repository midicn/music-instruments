姆比拉是一类以手指拨动金属簧片发声的乐器，属于体鸣乐器中的簧片类。金属条固定在木板或共鸣箱上，可再放入葫芦等共鸣体。马拉维与津巴布韦的相关制作和演奏传统以 Mbira/Sansi 名称列入联合国教科文组织的非物质文化遗产名录。

## 发声与结构

拨片释放后振动，能量经固定处传给木板和共鸣体。每片的有效长度、厚度、质量和安装方式共同影响音高与衰减。不同型号的簧片数量和排列并不相同，不能给所有姆比拉规定同一个音域或一种调式。

| 部件 | 观察重点 |
|---|---|
| 金属簧片 | 长短、宽厚、排列与可触及位置 |
| 固定结构 | 如何压紧簧片，怎样改变有效长度 |
| 木板或共鸣箱 | 声音如何传递和辐射 |
| 附加振响件 | 某些传统使用金属片等产生嗡鸣，不是所有型号都有 |

## 布局与循环

先按实际乐器标出音名，不要假设从左到右始终升高。某些布局把相邻音分到两手，有些按特定曲目和传统安排。把两手各自的音型分开练，再合起来听交错后的循环；同一段材料在速度、重音和分组改变后，会显出不同的层次。

## 听赏与使用

先分辨起音、持续部分、附加振响与重复周期，再看演奏者如何组织双手。文化用途应结合具体社群、场合和曲目理解，不能把所有拇指琴都解释成同一种仪式乐器。下方MIDI提供旋律与节奏材料，不能替代传统演奏录音；改编时应保留来源与所用调弦的信息。

Mbira refers to African instruments sounded by plucking metal tines, within the lamellophone group of idiophones. Tines are fixed to a board or sound box, sometimes used with an additional resonator. Related crafting and playing traditions in Malawi and Zimbabwe are inscribed by UNESCO as Mbira/Sansi.

## Structure and sound

A released tine vibrates and transfers energy through its mounting to the board and resonator. Effective length, thickness, mass and mounting affect pitch and decay. Tine counts, layouts and tunings vary; there is no single compass or scale covering every mbira.

| Part | What to inspect |
|---|---|
| Metal tines | Length, thickness, arrangement and reach |
| Mounting | How the tines are held and their effective length adjusted |
| Board or sound box | Transmission and radiation of sound |
| Additional rattles | Buzzing components present in some instruments, not all |

## Layout and cycles

Map the pitches on the actual instrument rather than assuming they rise continuously from left to right. Some layouts distribute adjacent pitches between the hands; others follow the requirements of a particular tradition. Practise each hand’s pattern, then hear the interlocking cycle. Changes of tempo, accent and grouping can reveal different layers.

## Listening and arranging

Identify attacks, sustain, buzzing and the repeating cycle before following the player’s hand organisation. Interpret cultural uses through the particular community, occasion and repertoire. Not every thumb piano is one kind of ritual instrument. MIDI can supply melodic or rhythmic material, but cannot replace documented traditional recordings. Preserve source and tuning information when arranging.
