## ⭐⭐ Bernoulli 不够，几何来凑

| 阶段 | 声门几何 | 气动力方向 |
|---|---|---|
| 开启相 | **收敛**（下闭上开） | 向外推 = 助力开启 |
| 闭合相 | **发散**（上闭下开） | 向内压 = 助力闭合 |

⭐⭐ **「Bernoulli forces……Not well at all, researchers have found.
Soon, oscillation would damp out」**——早期解释撑不住自持振荡；
Titze 1980 的修正让机制完备：⭐⭐ **「the aerodynamic force on the folds
is always slightly in phase with their motion——pushing them in whichever
direction they are already moving」——风永远顺着它推。**

⭐⭐ **黏膜波是几何的来源**：「a traveling surface wave……
inferior-to-superior direction」；「the body (thyroarytenoid muscle) is
mechanically stiffer than the cover (vocal ligament and mucosa)」——
⭐⭐ **「not a decorative feature of fold vibration but the tissue-mechanical
mechanism that makes self-sustained MEAD oscillation possible」。**

## ⭐⭐ 起振门槛：2–3 cmH₂O

⭐⭐ **「a minimum amount of lung pressure……roughly 2 to 3 centimeters
of water pressure——a very small force」**——**万事开头只差一口气。**

⭐⭐ **「If your vocal folds are dry, swollen, or stiff, that threshold rises——
this is why your voice feels effortful when you're dehydrated」**——
⭐⭐ **「嗓子哑了」的物理学是门槛升高，不是肺变小了。**

⭐⭐ **频率由一对对抗肌管理**：cricothyroid 拉长拉紧（提音高）·
thyroarytenoid 缩短放松（降音高）——**一台振荡器的粗调与细调。**
男声 85–180Hz · 女声 165–255Hz · 儿童约 250Hz。

## ⭐⭐ Singer's formant：穿透乐队的那道缝

> 「**a concentration of acoustic energy around 2,500 to 3,500 Hz——
> illustrates how the entire vocal system, not just the vocal cords,
> shapes the final output**」.

⭐⭐ **人声不与乐队争最响处（低频区），另开一个 2.5–3.5kHz 的
共振峰频段钻空档**——⭐⭐ **「为穿透而调」的特征：
训练与演化各做了一半。**

⭐⭐ **声质谱：同一台机器的两个故障方向**——
「breathy (excessive airflow with incomplete glottal closure) →
pressed (tight adduction with reduced airflow) → a balanced 『flow』
phonation」：**一个闭合不紧，一个气流不够——「好声音」是中间的流量平衡。**

⭐⭐ **对照全站的振荡源**：Telharmonium 的电 · Rhythmicon 的算 ·

## ⭐⭐ Bernoulli is not enough — geometry completes it

| Phase | Glottal geometry | Aerodynamic force |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | **convergent** (closed at bottom, open at top) | pushes outward — assists opening |
| Closing | **divergent** (the reverse) | presses inward — assists closing |

⭐⭐ **「Bernoulli forces……Not well at all, researchers have found. Soon,
oscillation would damp out」** — the early explanation could not sustain
self-sustained oscillation; Titze 1980's revision completed the mechanism:
⭐⭐ **「the aerodynamic force on the folds is always slightly in phase with
their motion — pushing them in whichever direction they are already
moving」 — the wind always pushes with it.**

⭐⭐ **The mucosal wave is the geometry's source**: 「a traveling surface
wave……inferior-to-superior direction」; 「the body (thyroarytenoid muscle)
is mechanically stiffer than the cover (vocal ligament and mucosa)」 —
⭐⭐ **「not a decorative feature of fold vibration but the tissue-mechanical
mechanism that makes self-sustained MEAD oscillation possible」.**

## ⭐⭐ The phonation threshold: 2–3 cmH₂O

⭐⭐ **「a minimum amount of lung pressure……roughly 2 to 3 centimeters of
water pressure — a very small force」** — **everything starts with one
breath.**

⭐⭐ **「If your vocal folds are dry, swollen, or stiff, that threshold rises —
this is why your voice feels effortful when you're dehydrated」** —
⭐⭐ **the physics of a hoarse voice is a raised threshold, not smaller lungs.**

⭐⭐ **Frequency is managed by an antagonistic pair**: the cricothyroid
elongates and tenses (raises pitch) · the thyroarytenoid shortens and relaxes
(lowers it) — **an oscillator's coarse and fine tuning.** Male 85–180Hz ·
female 165–255Hz · children about 250Hz.

## ⭐⭐ The singer's formant: the gap that pierces an orchestra

> 「**a concentration of acoustic energy around 2,500 to 3,500 Hz —
> illustrates how the entire vocal system, not just the vocal cords,
> shapes the final output**」.

⭐⭐ **The voice does not compete with the orchestra in its loudest region
(the low frequencies); it opens a 2,500–3,500Hz formant band elsewhere and
slips through the gap** — ⭐⭐ **tuned for penetration: training and evolution
did half each.**

⭐⭐ **The timbre spectrum: two failure directions of the same machine** —
「breathy (excessive airflow with incomplete glottal closure) → pressed
(tight adduction with reduced airflow) → a balanced 『flow』 phonation」:
**one closes too loosely, the other lacks airflow — a 「good voice」 is the
flow balance in between.**

## ⭐ The first mathematical model that 「sounds by itself」

⭐⭐ **The two-mass model (Ishizaka & Flanagan, 1972)**: 「each vocal fold
represented as two coupled masses……the lower mass opens and closes slightly
ahead of the upper」 — **the first model that could compute self-sustained
oscillation, hundreds of thousands of years after humans started singing.**

⭐⭐ **Against the site's oscillation sources**: the Telharmonium's electricity ·
the Rhythmicon's computation · the voice's wind — **three 「non-human」
oscillation sources, and wind is the oldest** (against 《大风吹来竹叶飞》:
wind scatters bamboo leaves; wind makes folds sound).
