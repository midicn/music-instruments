“说话鼓”指能模拟语言某些音高与节奏特征的鼓类，并非一个覆盖所有非洲鼓的名称。这里主要介绍西非常见的沙漏形压力鼓，以约鲁巴 dùndún 家族为例。两个鼓面通过张力绳相连，演奏者把鼓夹在手臂与躯干之间，击奏时挤压绳索改变张力和音高。

## 为什么可以模拟语言

约鲁巴语的音高、节奏和语调参与区分与组织话语。鼓可以模仿其中部分轮廓，但不能直接发出完整的辅音与元音。听者还会利用熟悉的表达、场合和上下文理解；有音高变化，不等于任何听者都能逐字听懂。

| 动作或条件 | 作用 |
|---|---|
| 挤压张力绳 | 改变鼓膜张力，使音高上升或下降 |
| 槌的击奏 | 组织起音、节奏和力度 |
| 连续改变压力 | 形成滑移的音高走向 |
| 固定表达与语境 | 为识别鼓语提供线索 |

## 音域不是固定键盘

具体音高范围随鼓径、鼓皮、调音和压力变化。低、中、高的相对位置可用于分析声调轮廓，却不意味着每支鼓固定只有三个音，也不意味着三档必定对应Do、Re、Mi。编配时应先了解实际乐器和演奏传统，再决定是否记为确定音高。

## 怎样听真实演奏

先跟随一个短句的高低走向，再听重复处是否改变节奏或延长某个音。把独奏、合奏中的领奏和与歌声呼应分开观察。若要解释某段鼓语的含义，应引用演奏者或相关语言社群的说明，不凭听感编译。下方MIDI适合观察节奏与音符安排，不承担传统鼓语翻译。

Talking drums imitate aspects of speech pitch and rhythm; the term does not identify all African drums. This entry focuses on West African hourglass pressure drums, using the Yorùbá dùndún family as an example. Tension cords connect two heads. The player holds the drum between arm and torso and changes tension by squeezing the cords while striking it.

## How speech is suggested

Pitch, rhythm and intonation help organise Yorùbá speech. A drum can imitate some contours without producing the full consonants and vowels. Familiar expressions, occasion and context also aid understanding. Changing pitch does not mean that any listener can decode each word.

| Action or condition | Role |
|---|---|
| Pressure on cords | Changes head tension and pitch |
| Stick strokes | Organise onsets, rhythm and dynamics |
| Continuous pressure changes | Produce gliding contours |
| Familiar expressions and context | Supply clues for interpretation |

## A compass rather than fixed keys

The available pitches depend on drum size, heads, tuning and pressure. Relative low, middle and high positions can describe tonal contours, but do not limit every drum to three notes or assign them fixed solfège syllables. Consult the actual instrument and tradition before scoring definite pitches.

## Listen to documented performances

Follow the contour of one short phrase, then notice rhythmic changes on repetition. Distinguish solo passages, ensemble leadership and responses to singing. For a phrase’s meaning, consult the performer or relevant language community rather than inventing a translation by ear. The MIDI examples can illustrate note and rhythm organisation, not translate traditional drum speech.
